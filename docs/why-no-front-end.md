# Why this desk has no front end

The recurring job is a weekly capacity decision, a record of the evidence and a list of owners. The agent reads and writes those records through one CLI. Read-only HTML briefs show the state in your business name and colours.

A live management screen offers streaming measurements, topology diagrams, mobile alerts and immediate shared updates. This base has none of those. Local PGlite has one process at a time; shared PostgreSQL is the option for concurrent users. Files stay offline once generated, except the optional hosted font and logo. There is no offline synchronization, drag and drop, remote control or identity management.

Enterprise DNA builds the connections and owner screens your estate needs. A sizing candidate is an internal review prompt. Operators test the change, use their infrastructure tooling to perform it, and record the observed outcome here.
